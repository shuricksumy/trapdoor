**Several admins, read-only accounts and an audit log**, all in a new Admin area.

## Accounts and roles

- Every person signing in with Authelia (or another OIDC provider) gets **their own account**
  at the first sign-in, named after them, instead of all sharing one. Its role follows their
  groups: `TD_OIDC_GROUPS` sign in as **admins**, the new `TD_OIDC_VIEWER_GROUPS` as
  **read-only**.
- Local accounts with a password still work (and stay the way in when the sign-on is down).
- A **read-only** account sees every page except Tools, with a banner at the top; the buttons
  that change something are hidden, settings forms are locked, and profiles, WireGuard configs
  and backup files cannot be downloaded. The API enforces the same: only reading, apart from
  logging out and changing its own password.
- There is always at least one admin who is turned on.

## Admin area

- **Manage** (⚙) in the server bar opens it: My account, **Admins**, Servers, Nodes, Backups,
  Notifications and the **Audit log**, as tabs.
- **Admins**: every account with its role, how it signs in, its last sign-in (when, from
  where, how) and its open sessions. Add a local account, change a role, turn an account off
  (its sessions end), set a new password (also a local one for a single sign-on account), log
  it out everywhere, delete it.
- **My account** is folded into sections like the Server page.

## Audit log

- Every change, sign-in and failed sign-in, with who, how they signed in and from where, now
  stored in the database (it was only in the log before), kept for two years.
- Filters by period, person, kind of action and text, pages of 15 / 25 / 50 / 100, and
  *Export CSV*.

## Also

- The server bar (server, where it runs, Manage) is a panel of its own.
- Overview on a phone: with a price per GB set, the five tiles ran off the screen; they now
  wrap into two columns.

## Upgrading from 0.8.x

`docker compose pull && docker compose up -d web`: only the web UI changed; the database is
upgraded on start.

If you sign in with OIDC: your next sign-in creates your own account (e.g. `anna`, admin)
next to the existing `admin`, which keeps its password. To keep the old behaviour, everyone as
one local account, set `TD_OIDC_USER=admin`. For read-only users, add their group to
`TD_OIDC_VIEWER_GROUPS` (and allow it in your provider's policy for trapdoor).
